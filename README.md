# 🚗 BMW Sales Case Study (2010–2024)

## 📘 Overview
This project presents a comprehensive analysis of BMW’s global sales performance between 2010 and 2024.  
It explores key trends, regional performance, market share evolution, and the impact of electric vehicles on BMW’s growth strategy.

## 🎯 Objectives
- Analyze BMW’s sales data and trends from 2010 to 2024  
- Identify factors influencing sales performance

- Provide insights and recommendations for future growth

## 🔍 Key Insights
- SUV and EV segments are the main drivers of BMW’s recent growth  
- Strongest performance observed in **Europe** and **China**  
- BMW’s **i-Series** (i4, iX, i7) shows rapid adoption  
- Focus on sustainability and electric mobility fuels long-term competitiveness  

---

## 🧠 Conclusion
BMW continues to innovate in design, technology, and sustainability, ensuring a strong position in the premium automotive segment.  
Future success will depend on how effectively BMW scales its electric and digital transformation strategies.
